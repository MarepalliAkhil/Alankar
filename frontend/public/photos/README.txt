Put your own photos here to replace the generated artwork, named after a slot:
salon-hero, spa-hero, parlor-hero, shop-background, salon-background, spa-background,
parlor-background. PNG, JPG, JPEG and WebP are all accepted (WebP is used first, then JPG, JPEG,
PNG). Then run "npm run photos:optimize" from the frontend folder to make small WebP and JPG
copies (under 200 KB); the originals move to frontend/photos-originals/. Details:
docs/IMAGE_SLOTS.md.
